Key Takeaways Anthropic has emerged as the leading enterprise AI lab, with ARR multiplying from $9 billion in December 2025 to more than $75 billion by July 2026. Claude Code is a major growth engine, monetizing a high-value workload where usage-based pricing ties AI spend directly to software-development productivity. Heavy investment in frontier-model training masks otherwise attractive business economics, and SemiAnalysis sees a clear path to profitability as the business scales.
