Key Takeaways Healthcare sits near a historic low in market ownership, even as the sector's growth profile strengthens, with 2027 earnings growth expected to trail only tech. Earnings growth, drug innovation, financing activity, and M&A all point to a sector investing for the future. Many investors remain underweight healthcare, while favorable seasonality around the midterm elections and emerging AI applications are among several near-term catalysts suggesting the sector deserves renewed attention.
